    THE PANEL

    App icon (.png) Request a demo

    Abstract

    We present AeroNexus, an Android device management panel from DigiInn360 for fleets that leave the building. One panel enrolls company phones, pins them to approved apps, switches hardware on and off, draws geofences, pushes files, shares the screen, and locks or wipes a phone that goes missing. Released with an audit log that records who did what, when and from where. Limitations include a heavy dependency on the phone being switched on, and on someone remembering to charge it.
