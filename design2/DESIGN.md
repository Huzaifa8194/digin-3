# /design2: "Sanctuary" (single-screen 3D story)

Built with `/awwwards`. One screen, one living particle field, a brand story in five acts.

## Concepts

| | Idea | Anchor | O / F / Fe / P |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Sanctuary | One field of light tells the story: the network storm, attacks streaming in, a 360° orbit that exposes the weak points, the particles closing into the DigiInn360 shield, then the brand name inside a 360° orbit | the logo's shield and the "360" in the name | 5 / 5 / 4 / 2 |
| B. The Inn | A lit 3D inn in a data storm | the name | 4 / 3 / 3 / 1 |
| C. Rotor | Cipher drums again | /design1 | 2 / 4 / 5 / 3 |

```
Idea:        Out of the noise, a shield: the story of what DigiInn360 does, told by one field of light.
Metaphor:    a storm that is organised into a sanctuary.
Signature:   ~60k GPU particles morphing between five formations, scroll-scrubbed camera, bloom.
Emotion:     awe first (the storm), then relief (the shield closes), then calm (the name).
Motion verb: gather. Chaos converges, swirls, and settles into form.
Screenshot:  act 4: the red shield of light with its white check, threats deflecting off its rim.
```

## Acts (scroll scrubs one timeline; nothing else on the page)

| Act | Formation | Camera | Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 The storm | turbulent volume, red threats inside | far, drifting | Every second, the network is noise. |
| 02 The way in | streams converging on the centre | closer | Every attack needs a way in. |
| 03 Every angle | a sphere, red weak points, scan ring | full 360° orbit | We look from every angle. |
| 04 The shield | the DigiInn360 shield with its check | front | Then we close the gaps. |
| 05 DigiInn360 | shield, wordmark and orbit ring | pull back | See risk. Stay ahead. |

Palette: night `#030407`, ink `#f4f6fa`, brand red `#db000a` / glow `#ff2b3a`, cold white particles.
Type: Manrope 800 for the lines, JetBrains Mono for the act counter. Reduced motion: a still of act 5
with all five lines readable as text.
