What does cybersecurity protect?

Cybersecurity helps defend systems, networks and information against unauthorized access, theft and disruption.

Why does cybersecurity matter for businesses?

It helps safeguard sensitive information, maintain operations and reduce the financial and reputational effects of an attack.

Which cyber threats should we be aware of?

Phishing, ransomware, malware and denial-of-service attacks can affect businesses. Application risks also include injection attacks and stolen credentials.

How can we strengthen our defenses?

Combine secure access, updated software, network protection and staff awareness. Discuss a security review to understand your priorities.
