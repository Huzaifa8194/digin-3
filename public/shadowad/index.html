01 / The engagement

From foothold
to verified closure.

Follow an engagement through four stages, from the first working attack path to a retest that verifies the fix.

01Exploit

Open-source engines run multi-step attack chains from an initial foothold to compromise, within the engagement’s approved scope.

02Prove

ShadowAD captures timestamped evidence as testing happens and feeds it into the report.

03Fix

Check who still relies on the affected access before applying a fix. Each fix pack includes a rollback snapshot.

04Retest

Replay the attack to verify the fix and record dated proof that the route to Domain Admin is closed.
