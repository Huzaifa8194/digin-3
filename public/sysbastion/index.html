01 / Control index

Choose what to harden.

Build a baseline around the controls your environment needs. The capabilities below describe the planned feature set.

01Windows security and privacy

Manage AutoRun and AutoPlay, SmartScreen, LSA protection and selected mitigations. Configure selected telemetry, usage-data and automatic app installation settings.

02File types and scripting

Restrict selected script, disk-image and installer types, including JS, VBS, ISO, IMG and MSI. Manage Windows Script Host and selected PowerShell settings, including script-block logging where configured.

03Services and built-in apps

Disable selected services and features and remove built-in Windows apps your organization does not need.

04Application security

Restrict selected Office macro, OLE and ActiveX features and configure Adobe Reader security options.

05Firewall policy

Manage inbound and outbound rules for selected system utilities, Windows apps, .NET, Office and Adobe software.
