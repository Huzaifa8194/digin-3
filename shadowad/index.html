Responsible use

////// Before any engagement, all of these are true

  • Written authorization on file. Documented permission from the asset owner, with scope, dates and signatory, stored with the run.
  • Testing stays in scope. Only declared targets are touched; everything else is refused.
  • A human approves harmful actions. Destructive and credential-touching steps happen behind operator confirmation.
  • Nothing persists beyond the engagement. Any exception is separately authorized, tracked and cleaned up.
  • Data is protected and disposed of. Evidence is confidential, encrypted at rest, and purged per the engagement agreement.
  • A person reviews the output. Reports are checked by a human before anything goes to a client.

/// The one-line test

Would this look like a tester proving a fixable weakness on assets they are allowed to touch? shadowAD is designed, every time, to be exactly that.
